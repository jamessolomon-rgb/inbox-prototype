Three designs

The item view you get after tapping Start. Each mock-up runs the same three examples: an email with a calendar clash, a text with a task and an event, and a family group chat. Swipe or use the arrows to move between them. Made-up data; nothing is sent.

A. Brief first

The latest message in full at the top. Older messages folded away. Suggestions as one-line rows under the message, with the words they came from highlighted. Long emails and group chats show a summary instead.
